Read the Game

Watch a hockey game and see what the numbers are made of

Hockey is fast, and most of what decides a game is invisible unless someone points at it. This replays one real game slowly enough to follow, and shows its work: every count is traceable to a recorded event, and every event it leaves out says why.

What this does and does not claim

  • One game, not a season.Everything here is MIN at BUF, 10 November 2023. A single game cannot tell you what is normal, so nothing here claims to.
  • A replay, not live coverage.The game is over. Nothing on this site fetches anything while you watch it — the events were pulled once, checked, and built into the page.
  • Nothing is modelled or invented.Every mark traces to a recorded event. There is no expected-goals number, because that would be our estimate presented as the game's fact.
  • The counting is shown, including what it drops.Each layer accounts for all 320 events in the game: the ones it counts, and the ones it excludes with the reason. The totals have to reconcile.
