Read the Game

Every night in the archive

Three seasons of NHL hockey, night by night. Pick a date to see the games played on it.

Game data from the NHL's public play-by-play feed, fetched once and stored. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of the National Hockey League or any team. No NHL or team logos, wordmarks or crests appear anywhere on this site — teams are named in plain text, and identified by colour and three-letter abbreviation.

Source on GitHub — every number here is a function of stored events, and you can read the function. Found one that looks wrong? ReadTheGameOfHockey@gmail.com
