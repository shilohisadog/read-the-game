Read the Game

Every night in the archive

Three seasons of NHL hockey, night by night. Pick a date to see the games played on it.

Game data from the NHL's public play-by-play feed, fetched once and stored. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a product of the National Hockey League or any club. No NHL or club logos, wordmarks or crests appear anywhere on this site — teams are identified by colour and three-letter abbreviation only.

Source on GitHub — every number here is a function of stored events, and you can read the function. Found one that looks wrong? ReadTheGameOfHockey@gmail.com

Nothing here is paywalled. If it helped you read a game, you can buy me a coffee.
