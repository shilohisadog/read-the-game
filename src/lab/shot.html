What's recorded, and drawn: where the shot was taken from, whether it was saved or went in, the shot type, the shooter's team, number, height and which way he shoots, and who was in net.

What isn't recorded, and isn't drawn: the puck's path, height or speed, where it crossed the line, screens, and where the goaltender stood. So the dashed line shows only the direction toward the net and fades out at the crease, and the moment cuts from the release to the outcome. The stick motion is the general motion of that shot type, not this player's.
