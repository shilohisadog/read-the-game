A goal, from the league's puck and player tracking

What's real here. Every dot is a position the NHL's puck and player tracking recorded, 10 times a second: each skater, each goaltender and the puck. Between those tenths nothing is drawn, and the puck's dashed trail joins only its own recorded positions. Names and the score come from the league's play-by-play, as on the replay.

What's not. Tracking exists only around goals (about 14 seconds each). The file is the league's (wsr.nhle.com/sprites/…), fetched once and stored here for this demo.
