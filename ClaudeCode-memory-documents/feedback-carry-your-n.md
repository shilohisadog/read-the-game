CHENG, 2026-09-01, on my penalties audit correcting my own conversational
report from two turns earlier:

> *"That's the same shape as the 4× headline that turned out to be 8-game noise.
> Twice now in short order, which suggests the discipline is right and the HABIT
> isn't yet — **any number offered in conversation should carry its n out loud**,
> because a bare figure gets remembered as a fact about hockey and then designed
> against."*

## What I actually did
Kevin asked whether the penalties card should get a diagram. I measured **the
reference game alone** and told him:

> *"The gap is zero seconds in three of four, and the goalie is never pulled."*

Both halves are artifacts of that one game. Measured properly (46-game stratified
sample, 109 delayed→penalty pairs) the gap is **never** exactly zero — median 4s,
p90 28s, max 91s — and a goalie change shows in `sit` on **30.3%**.

## ⭐ WHY IT IS WORSE THAN AN ORDINARY MISTAKE
He had already started designing against it. A number in prose has no error bars
attached to it and no population; by the time it reaches the next turn it is a
premise. **The correction cost more than the measurement would have.**

## The rule
State the n, the population and the date **in the same sentence as the number**,
in conversation and not only in the artifact. *"3 of 4 in the reference game"* is
honest; *"three of four"* is not. Where the n is 1, say so before saying the
number at all.

⚠️ The discipline already exists in the written artifacts — every doc in `docs/`
carries its population. **This is about the turns in between**, which is where
the two failures both happened.
