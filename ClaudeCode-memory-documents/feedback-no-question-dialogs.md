**Kevin, 2026-08-09: "I don't care for those 'Ask a Question' dialog boxes, by the way."**

He rejected an `AskUserQuestion` call outright and answered in prose instead — and his prose answer was better than any of the options I had written, because he described the behaviour he wanted rather than picking from a menu I had framed.

**Why:** the dialog forces a decision into pre-set choices at the moment I happen to ask. Kevin thinks by talking the problem through, and the framing itself is often the thing worth arguing with. A multiple-choice box makes the framing invisible and un-arguable. It also fits [[feedback-discuss-in-thread]]: decisions get made in conversation, not in a widget.

**How to apply:** never call `AskUserQuestion` on this project. When a decision is genuinely his, lay out the options as prose in the thread — the evidence, the trade-off, a recommendation — and let him respond however he wants. Keep making a recommendation ([[feedback-keep-building]]); just don't box the reply.
