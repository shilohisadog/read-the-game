On 2026-09-17, after a long point-by-point reply weighing CHENG's review (agreements, four objections,
a proposed gate, an ordering), Kevin: *"that's alot of words, can you condense into a clear plan of
action, please."* The condensed reply — five numbered steps, one line each, then "Your decision:" —
was approved as written ("approved as described").

**Why:** Kevin directs and approves; long argument is useful when he is weighing, but when the next
move is his approval he needs the plan, not the reasoning. The reasoning still belongs in the repo
docs, where it is recorded after he converges.

**How to apply:** when a reply ends in something Kevin must approve or choose, lead with a numbered
plan (≤ ~5 steps, one line each) and a single explicit decision line. Keep the evidence and
counter-arguments short or offer them on request. Still carry every n ([[feedback-carry-your-n]])
and still argue the opposite where it matters ([[feedback-argue-the-opposite]]) — just briefly.
