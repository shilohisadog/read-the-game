Use the commit hash if you want the full story: `git show <hash>`.

For each entry, fill in the three answers after the `→`. One letter each; `?` is a fine answer.

- **Real?** `y` something was factually wrong · `n` a design call or preference, not a defect · `?` unsure
- **Found by** `K` you, looking at or asking about the site · `M` CC or CHENG reviewing or measuring · `A` an automated check (tests, CI, a browser check, a mutation, a pipeline alarm) · `?` can't tell
